Objects are created through collections: doc.layers.add(), layer.pathItems.rectangle(), doc.textFrames.add(). Old JavaScript: no map/forEach/filter." (this absorbs the `b.map` entry below). - **Illustrator / After Effects: "The … panel isn't connected"** (USER STATE, maybe BUG). Twenty-two times this week, up from 20: Illustrator on MSKMAC14 on 29 Oct (3), 30 Oct (9) and 2 Nov (4, between 14:21 and 15:08 in the middle of a working session); Illustrator on MSKMAC37 on 2 Nov (4, each between scripts that worked) and 3 Nov 10:52 (1); and, new, After Effects on MSKMAC37 on 3 Nov 12:02 (1), the first time an After Effects panel dropped. Both apps use the same kind of panel (CEP), so one cause fits all: the CEP panel ([engine/cep/com.mikechambers.ai/main.js](engine/cep/com.mikechambers.ai/main.js) and its After Effects twin) relies on Socket.IO's automatic reconnect, and a panel that the app collapses or unloads stops running. Two different Macs losing the panel mid-session points at the software, not the user. Fix to find out which: in [hub.js](hub.js) the panel `disconnect` handler (line 1769) ignores Socket.IO's reason; log and count it ("transport close" = network/hub, "client namespace disconnect" / "ping timeout" = the panel itself) and send that count with usage, so next week says whether it is the user or the software. Second step if it is the software: have the hub ask the app to reopen the panel when a command finds it gone, as it already does at launch (2.55 change). Not in source yet.

## Fixed, awaiting release

- **Premiere/Media Encoder: the hidden After Effects engine crashes every ~10 s** (BUG, critical). Reported by Ale on 4 Nov with 100+ crash reports from 2–4 Nov; two more on this Mac on 2 Nov. Dynamic Link starts `aerendercore` (a windowless After Effects) to show AE comps. It ran our Scripts/Startup script, whose `app.executeCommand` crashed it in `CEggApp::UpdateMenus`, so the comps went offline. Fixed in [hub.js](hub.js) `aeStartupScript` and [panel-starter.html](panel-starter.html): neither does anything when `app.isRenderEngine` is true or the app path contains aerendercore. The hub rewrites the script at every launch, so updating fixes it. Tested on this Mac on 4 Nov with a Premiere project holding an AE comp and After Effects closed: 6 crashes a minute before, none after, and the comp rendered. After Effects still opens and connects its panel at launch. Released in 2.58.0 as a pre-release; reaches the apps once promoted.

- **Photoshop `create_document` fails on Italian Photoshop** (BUG). Last seen 29 Oct 13:46, MSKMAC14 (2.55.0), twice, a few hours before the fix shipped. It looked for the layer "Background", which Italian Photoshop calls "Sfondo", so it threw after making the document and Claude retried into a second one. Fixed with a build.sh patch (uses the document's own background layer and passes the name). Released in 2.56.0 on 29 Oct. Nobody on 2.56+ has created a Photoshop document since (the only Photoshop use this week came from the Macs stuck on 2.52 and 2.53, which still have the bug; no Photoshop use at all since 2 Nov). Closes once a 2.57 Mac creates a document and the error does not come back.
- **A reinstall gives the Mac a new usage id** (BUG). MSKMAC14 went from 2.55.0 to 2.57.0 on 30 Oct and came back as a new install (ff84b1… became 3bca1f…), so the usage page shows it as two Macs. The old uninstaller (up to 2.55) wiped the whole support folder, id included; since 2.56 [uninstall.sh](uninstall.sh) keeps analytics.json. MSKMAC37's 2.50 → 2.57 self-update on 2 Nov kept its id, as expected (self-updates never lost it). Closes when a Mac reinstalls from 2.56+ and keeps its id, or after a quiet month.
